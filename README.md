
# 🏙️ City Skyline

**Build a city skyline one shop at a time. One tap. One tower. How high can you go?**

A simple, addictive stacking game where precision is everything. A crane swings each storefront across the sky. Tap to drop it onto your tower. Land it perfectly and keep your tower wide. Miss the alignment and the overhang gets sliced away, leaving a narrower shop for your next drop.

Miss completely, and your shop tumbles to the street. Game over.

Easy to learn. Hard to master.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Tap anywhere** to release the swinging shop.
- Line it up with the shop below to keep your tower wide.
- Land **four perfect drops in a row** and your shop grows wider again.
- The crane gets faster as your tower gets taller.
- Keep stacking and beat your personal best!

Precision is the key. Every drop matters.

## 🌆 A Sky That Changes as You Climb

Your tower isn't just getting taller. The entire atmosphere changes as you progress.

- Start building in beautiful daylight.
- Watch the sky transform into a golden sunset.
- Keep climbing into the night.
- Every perfect drop in a streak plays a higher musical note, creating one continuous melody.
- Feel a light haptic tap with every perfect drop.

The higher you climb, the more rewarding the experience becomes.
